FAQ

Questions customers usually ask.

If your question isn't here, call or WhatsApp us and we'll help you understand the process.

What is an ELV?

ELV means End-of-Life Vehicle — a vehicle that has reached the end of its useful life or is no longer suitable or economical to keep in service.

Which vehicles do you accept?

We currently invite enquiries for cars, two-wheelers and commercial vehicles. Availability and eligibility can depend on the vehicle, location and applicable requirements.

Can you pick up a non-running vehicle?

Pickup options depend on the vehicle condition, location and available arrangements. Include the condition in your quote request so the team can advise you.

How is the scrap value decided?

Valuation can depend on factors such as vehicle type, condition, weight/materials, location and applicable market or processing considerations. A final amount should be confirmed with the team.

What documents will I need?

Documentation requirements can vary. Share your vehicle details first and the team can explain the documents and process applicable to your case.

Do you handle accident-damaged vehicles?

Enquiries are welcome. Tell us the vehicle condition and location so we can assess whether the service is available for your case.
